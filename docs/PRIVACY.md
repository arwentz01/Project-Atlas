# Atlas privacy and data handling

Atlas is a workforce operations system. Collect only information needed for employment scheduling, credentials, timekeeping, and operational communication. Do not enter patient names, medical record numbers, diagnoses, treatment details, or employee diagnoses.

Organization administrators are responsible for access reviews, retention rules, lawful exports, and responding to workforce data requests. Portable JSON and CSV exports must be stored securely and deleted when no longer needed. Offboarding should deactivate access while retaining scheduling and audit history required by organizational policy.
