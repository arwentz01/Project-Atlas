# Feature Catalog

## Accounts and organizations

- Email or federated sign-in
- Individual professional profiles
- Organization creation and onboarding
- Staff invitations and delegated administration
- Role-based access control
- Organization branding profile
- Logo, contact block, and approved footer configuration
- Organization resource library
- Personal drafts and saved items
- Organization, regional, and public sharing scopes

## Knowledge and content management

- Structured content types rather than unclassified articles
- Content categories, tags, specialties, payer, jurisdiction, and audience metadata
- Draft, review, approved, published, superseded, and archived states
- Version history and comparison
- Effective, review, expiration, and superseded dates
- Source provenance and reviewer attribution
- Related-content links
- Corrections and feedback workflow
- Scheduled review queues
- Content ownership and stewardship assignments

## Official source ingestion

- Upload or import approved public documents
- Preserve original document and metadata
- Document text extraction
- Page and section citation anchors
- Candidate requirement extraction
- Payer, plan, jurisdiction, service, and equipment classification
- Change detection between document versions
- Human validation before publication
- Mapping from source language to structured requirements
- Source lifecycle and archival rules

## Search and guided discovery

- Full-text search
- Structured filters
- Synonyms and clinical terminology mapping
- Search by payer, topic, equipment, form, specialty, audience, or geography
- Concise answer cards with visible sources
- Guided question flows that narrow a workflow
- Recently viewed and saved searches
- Search feedback and missing-content reports
- Role-aware results
- Organization-specific ranking

## Workflow builder

- Reusable workflow templates
- Ordered steps and checklists
- Required documentation lists
- Forms and source attachments
- Role or owner assignments
- Conditional branches
- Warnings, exceptions, and common pitfalls
- Related patient materials
- Local organization overlays
- Workflow versioning
- Printable and shareable workflow views

## Patient education and resources

- Modern, non-branded global templates
- Organization-branded rendering
- Medication and injection teaching materials
- Disease, procedure, and aftercare handouts
- Blood-pressure, blood-glucose, symptom, medication, and intake/output logs
- Reading level, audience, and language metadata
- Accessibility-friendly HTML
- Print-quality PDF output
- Resource customization within controlled fields
- Organization-saved variants
- Global-source update notifications for forked resources
- Packet builder

## Clinical skills reference

- Purpose and scope
- Supplies and equipment
- Preparation
- Step-by-step technique
- Safety notes and contraindications
- Common errors and troubleshooting
- When to stop or escalate
- Diagrams and media where licensing permits
- Source references
- Organization policy attachments
- Quick-reference print view

## Laboratory learning reference

- Test purpose and specimen type
- Typical reference-range context and variability notes
- Common associations with high or low results
- Related tests and pattern links
- Limitations and interfering factors
- Learning pearls
- Escalation and urgency flags framed conservatively
- Professional-level variants for students, nurses, and advanced clinicians
- References and review dates

## Referral and resource directory

- Organizations, clinicians, vendors, and community services
- Insurance participation claims with source and verification date
- Geography and service-area filters
- Referral requirements
- Scheduling and intake notes
- Organization-maintained preferred resources
- Community-reported experiences
- Confidence, corroboration, and freshness indicators
- Correction and ownership claims
- Expiration and reverification queues

## Community knowledge

- Structured contributions rather than unrestricted comments
- Personal, organization, regional, and public visibility
- Experience versus verified-fact labeling
- Moderation workflow
- Contributor reputation and expertise context
- Corroboration counts
- Timestamps and automatic expiration
- Conflict and dispute handling
- De-identification reminders and content scanning

## Notifications

- Source document changed
- Global resource updated after an organization forked it
- Content due for review
- Organization invitation or role change
- Contribution approved, rejected, or needs revision
- Saved topic has material changes
- Policy or workflow superseded

## Administration and analytics

- User and role management
- Content review queues
- Source health dashboard
- Search analytics
- Zero-result searches
- Resource usage and reuse
- Packet-generation counts
- Content freshness and review debt
- Correction trends
- Audit-event explorer
- Data export for organization administrators

- Cloud storage and document-source connectors
- Translation workflows
